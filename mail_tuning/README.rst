===========
Mail Tuning
===========

Small usability fixes for Discuss (module ``mail``).

* The notification tab of the messaging menu (systray) is labelled "Messages"
  instead of "Notifications". The German "Benachrichtigungen" is wider than the
  tab bar and makes it scroll; "Nachrichten" fits.
